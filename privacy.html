Pocket Pet Focus – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026
The short version: Pocket Pet Focus does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Everything the app saves (your child's pet, quests, progress and settings) stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

Who we are

Pocket Pet Focus is a focus-and-routine app for kids, built around a virtual pet. It is published by Matthew Magallanes (the "developer", "we", "us").

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect, or transmit personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.

The app lets a parent enter a child's first name or nickname and a pet name so the app can address them. These names, along with quests, timers, progress, coins, items, and settings, are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or using the "Start over" option in the grown-up settings, deletes this data.

Children's privacy

Pocket Pet Focus is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families policy by design: we do not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • The "Pet chat" feature uses pre-written, tap-to-choose answers only. There is no free-text typing, no artificial-intelligence service, and nothing a child selects leaves the device.
  • The app contains no advertising and no links to outside websites or social media from the child-facing screens.
  • Settings that affect the child's experience are behind a parent passcode.

Purchases

If the app offers an optional in-app purchase (for example, unlocking all levels and features), the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google's handling of purchase data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Purchases require the parent passcode inside the app and are also subject to the purchase controls you set in Google Play.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, files, or any other device permission. It may ask for permission to show notifications only if you turn on quest reminders in the grown-up settings; notifications are generated on the device and contain no personal data.

Hosting and technical data

The app's content is delivered from a website hosted by GitHub Pages and cached on your device so the app works offline. Like any web host, GitHub may automatically record standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type) in its server logs when content is downloaded. We do not have access to, and do not use, these logs. GitHub's practices are described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Data sharing and selling

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any data, because we do not collect any.

Data security and retention

All app data lives on your device and is protected by your device's own security (screen lock, encryption). We retain nothing, because nothing is sent to us. You can erase all app data at any time by using "Start over" in the grown-up settings or by uninstalling the app.

Your choices

  • Delete all data: grown-up settings → Start over, or uninstall the app.
  • Change or remove names: grown-up settings → Names & code.
  • Turn reminders off: grown-up settings, or your device's notification settings.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles data, we will update this page and change the effective date above. Material changes that involve collecting any personal information would be announced in the app and would require a new version from Google Play.
