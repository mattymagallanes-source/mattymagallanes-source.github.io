Pocket Pet Focus Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

The short version: Pocket Pet Focus does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. Everything your child does in the app stays on your device.

Who we are

Pocket Pet Focus is made by Matthew Magallanes. You can reach us at matty.magallanes@gmail.com.

What the app stores

The app saves game progress only on the device where it is installed. This includes the pet's name and look, the child's first name or nickname if one is entered, quests and schedules a parent sets up, timers, coins, items, and messages typed into the pet chat. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

The parent code is stored on the device in a scrambled form so it is not shown in plain text.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or ask for an email address, phone number, location or photos.
  • We do not use advertising, ad tracking or advertising IDs.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting tools.
  • We do not sell or share any information.
  • The pet chat runs entirely on the device. Messages are not sent to any server or AI service.

Fonts

The app loads its two typefaces (Fredoka and Nunito) from Google Fonts the first time it opens with an internet connection. When that happens, Google receives the standard technical information any website request includes, such as the device's IP address. No game data is sent. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Children's privacy

Pocket Pet Focus is designed for children and their parents. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not collect personal information from children under 13. Parents control the quest schedule and settings through a parent code.

Deleting data

All data can be erased at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in the device settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and the updated policy will be available in the app and on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to matty.magallanes@gmail.com.
